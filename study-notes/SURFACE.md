# Study reader

Mode: Read. The user studies long technical chapters at a desk and on smaller screens.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A straightforward technical publication: a compact contents page and a clean article layout. The user rejected the first version's generic marketing copy and decorative notebook styling.

OWN-WORLD: White background, neutral ink, blue text links, self-hosted Source Sans 3, light gray code examples, fine gray rules. No slogan or decorative book identity.

STORY: Read a chapter, then open its separately numbered practice subchapter before proceeding to the next chapter. Book practice questions appear first, followed by the extra quiz. Both subsections independently save and score answers, immediately show explanations and green/red feedback, and support retry/reset. Reviewed corrections to the source book are labeled beside affected questions. Reading pages retain only a link to book practice.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A small masthead and Chapters menu above a centered article with a right section index. The chapter drawer opens on demand on every screen. Phones use an inline contents disclosure. The index starts with a factual title, short description, search, and an ordered chapter list.

FORM: Static document reader, with a searchable chapter index. Explicit user references guide the composition; seed: none (unavailable).
